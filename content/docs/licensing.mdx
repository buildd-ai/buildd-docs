# Licensing

buildd is **source-available** under the Functional Source License, Version 1.1, Apache 2.0 Future License (FSL-1.1-ALv2), a Fair Source license. It covers buildd's server (dashboard, API, control plane) in [buildd-ai/buildd](https://github.com/buildd-ai/buildd).

The runner, ai-kit and the contract packages are Apache-2.0. The paid offer is the hosted service at [buildd.dev](https://buildd.dev).

## Can I self-host it for my own team?

Yes. You can run buildd on your own infrastructure for your own work. See [Self-Hosting](/docs/deployment/self-hosting).

## Can I modify it?

Yes. You can fork it, patch it and extend it.

## Can I sell it as a hosted service or a competing product?

No, not during the two-year window. The license allows any use except offering a commercial product or service that competes with buildd.

## When does a release become Apache-2.0?

Two years after that release ships. Each release converts on its own date.

## What is Apache-2.0 now?

- The runner (`apps/runner`), which you install on your own machines
- `@buildd/workflow-kernel`
- `@buildd/spec-contract`
- `@buildd/dispatch-contract`
- `@buildd/shared`
- `@builddai/ai-kit`

## How do I contribute?

Code contributions require a CLA. You sign it once, on your first pull request.
